## Shared MYSTERY principles
The current ambient mystery entries intentionally carry narrator-improvisation prompts. Therefore their **current ambient form is optional and non-critical**.

A mystery may become story-critical only through a separate authored Storylet variant that supplies:
- actual graph truth,
- setup/signal,
- a tracked reveal condition,
- phase/expiry rules,
- explicit outcome/world effect.

Narrator improvisation may add flavor, optional hints or side discoveries. It may not invent:
- a mandatory story key,
- a new required route,
- a critical clue not present in graph truth,
- a contradiction of known state.

# Cross-bank findings from MYSTERY review

## 1. The current six mystery entries are deliberately ambient
All six current entries carry explicit improvisation framing. Therefore the safest rule is:

**current ambient instance != future authored critical variant**

A subject may support both, but they are distinct Storylets with different truth contracts.

## 2. Subject identity can be reused without leaking improvised truth into graph truth
For example `BOKA SOM SKRIVER TILBAKE` may appear as:
- ambient improv in one adventure,
- authored clue carrier in another.

The subject stays recognizable; the Storylet declares whether truth is authored or improvisable.

## 3. Knowledge reveal needs phase validity
Especially for:
- Den flyvende skoen,
- Stjernene i taket,
- Speilet som lyver.

An otherwise coherent clue can still be narratively wrong if it arrives after its dramatic function has expired.

## 4. Optional ambient mysteries must not quietly become progression pressure
They should remain safely ignorable. UI/copy should not imply that every mystery must be solved before moving on.

## 5. No random generic puzzle attachment
A future authored puzzle variant is contextual to the subject/story. Structural puzzle-template compatibility alone is insufficient.
